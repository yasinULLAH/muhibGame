Pilot's Field Guide

Controls
Tap screen, Left-Click, Space, Up Arrow, or W to flap. Press P / Esc to pause, M to mute, R to quick-restart.
Combo & Fever Mode
Chain 5 coins or Close Calls (passing within 12px of a pipe) to trigger 7s of 2× Score Fever Mode!
Power-Ups (Arcade & Blitz)
Shield absorbs 1 crash • Slow-Mo slows time • Magnet pulls coins • 2× doubles points • Tiny shrinks hitbox • Ruby Gem gives +5 coins.
Second Chance Revive
Use a Revive Heart on Game Over to clear nearby pipes and continue your run once per flight.
Zen Practice Mode
Softly bounces off pipes and ground instead of ending the flight — ideal for learning timing stress-free.
